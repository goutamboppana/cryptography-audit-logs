HOW IT WORKS

One event.
Three checks.

01
Canonical event

Each change is serialized consistently with its actor, timestamp, record, and before/after values.

02
Linked digest

SHA-256 binds the event to the previous entry, forming a sequence that reveals edits and broken links.

03
Keyed proof

HMAC-SHA-256 authenticates each digest with a secret key stored outside the database.

ⓘ

Hashes provide integrity checks, not encryption. A trusted checkpoint is needed to detect removal of the newest entries.
